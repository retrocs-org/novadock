# PET plugin

Add **PET** from Add item > Plugins for a small animated companion on its own
tile. Choose the **Character** in Edit settings:

| Character | Idle | Napping | When clicked |
|-----------|------|---------|--------------|
| **Cat** (Whiskers) | sways its tail, blinks, purrs | curls up with drifting Zs | hops with a heart |
| **Gerald** | a grumpy stapler who frowns and grumbles "Hmph." | snores with his jaw shut | chomps, fires a staple, and offers a piece of unsolicited advice |
| **Dog** (Biscuit) | wags its tail | sleeps with its head on its paws | hops with its tongue out |
| **Rabbit** (Clover) | twitches its nose and ears | naps as a loaf, ears laid back | jumps for joy |
| **Bob** | a polka-dot rubber ball who bounces and squashes | rests on the floor | leaps and chatters |

A click also wakes a napping pet. Gerald and Bob say something different
on each click ("Paper jam? Not my department.", "Gravity is a suggestion.").

Edit settings also sets the pet's name (shown with its mood as the tile's
value; leave it as Whiskers to use the character's own name), Slow, Normal
or Fast animation, and how many minutes (1–30) pass before it naps. Use
applies settings for the session; Save keeps them after restart.

PET opens no window and uses no sound or network. The dock repaints only the
PET tile for each frame. Napping halves the frame rate, and the animation
slows to an occasional update while the dock is hidden or covered, so an idle
PET costs very little CPU time.
